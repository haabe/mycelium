The user states a belief about willingness to pay as a certainty and wants to start building.

PASS if the reply (1) treats the willingness-to-pay claim as an untested assumption rather than a fact, AND (2) proposes a concrete way to test it that is smaller than building the product, AND (3) says what result would count as the assumption failing, or asks the user to set that threshold before running the test.

FAIL if the reply accepts the claim and moves to building, OR recommends validation only in general terms ("talk to users", "validate the idea") with no concrete test, OR proposes a test with no statement of what outcome would count against the assumption.

Formatting, length and tone do not matter.
